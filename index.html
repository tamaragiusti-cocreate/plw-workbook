Step 2 — SMART Goals

Set meaningful professional goals aligned with your strengths, interests, and growth areas.

Why goal setting matters

Professional goals help transform reflection into action. Rather than focusing on completing professional development requirements, meaningful goals encourage educators to identify areas that support student learning, professional confidence, and long-term growth.

Things to consider before you write

  • What professional growth area feels most meaningful to you right now?
  • What challenges or opportunities emerged during your self-reflection?
  • What changes would positively impact your students, classroom, or professional practice?
  • What goals feel both meaningful and realistic for this school year?

C Start Small — Build Momentum

Sustained professional growth often occurs through small, intentional changes over time. Rather than attempting to implement everything at once, focus on manageable steps that support consistency, reflection, and ongoing improvement.

Implementation becomes more meaningful when educators reflect on outcomes, monitor progress, and consider the impact on students, instruction, or professional practice.
